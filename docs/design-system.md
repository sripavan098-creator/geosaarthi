# GeoSaarthi Visual System

## Reference interpretation

The uploaded reference uses a quiet editorial research aesthetic rather than a conventional glossy dashboard. Its defining traits are a warm off-white canvas, near-black ink typography, high-contrast serif display headings, compact sans-serif body text, thin neutral dividers, careful whitespace, restrained grey surfaces, and sparse emphasis rather than bright decorative colour.

GeoSaarthi will translate that language into an analysis workspace. The resulting interface will be primarily warm-white and charcoal, with muted stone and mist surfaces for grouping. An orbital blue will identify active analysis and data states, while a restrained copper accent will signal warnings or important evidence. Cards will use subtle borders and small radius rather than heavy shadows. Typography will use an editorial serif for page titles and a legible sans-serif for dense metadata, forms, and traces.

## Interaction pattern

The workspace will use a persistent, compact left navigation on desktop and a top-level mobile switcher on small screens. Analysis proceeds through a visible sequence: choose mode, select a benchmark or upload a supported image, inspect validation, ask a question, and review evidence. Status is expressed through text and colour together. Hover and focus changes will be subtle, quick, and accessible; the product will not use distracting motion or performative AI effects.
