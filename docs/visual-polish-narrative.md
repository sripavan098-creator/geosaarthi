# GeoSaarthi Visual-Polish Narrative

## Design Intent

GeoSaarthi should read as a professional Earth-observation workspace rather than a decorative science-fiction interface. The visual story begins with the **Earth** as the analyst’s subject, moves through the **satellite** as the observation link, enters the **AI pipeline** as a visible decision system, resolves into **evidence** as proof, exposes the **execution trace** as accountable method, and concludes with the **report** as the mission artifact. Motion must clarify state changes, hierarchy, and provenance; it must not imply a live orbital service or unverified model output.

| Product concept | Visual role | Primary interaction | Motion principle | Truthfulness boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Earth | Central scientific object and contextual anchor | Select an area, layer, or benchmark scene | Slow orbital drift, restrained camera/imagery movement | Labels identify benchmark and demo imagery rather than live observation |
| Satellite | Link between scene and captured observation | Enter a mission or inspect acquisition context | Purposeful orbit and acquisition sweep | Telemetry remains explicitly simulated |
| AI pipeline | Visible reasoning system | Inspect routing and specialist selection | Progression advances one stage at a time | Every stage displays workflow metadata, not hidden reasoning claims |
| Evidence | Proof for each interpretation | Reveal, inspect, compare, or zoom a marked region | Focused pulse and image framing | Evidence remains a bounded visual reference, not calibrated spatial measurement |
| Trace | Transparent black box | Replay ordered execution events | Subtle chronological progress and latency emphasis | Trace describes scripted or recorded prototype execution accurately |
| Report | Final mission artifact | Review, export, and hand off findings | Panels resolve into a clean artifact, then stabilize | Export includes confidence, provenance, limitations, and metadata-only policy |

## Interaction Hierarchy

Each major surface requires a clear hero moment, one dominant analyst action, and a corresponding evidence state. Large negative space is reserved for the Earth view, imagery workbench, and final report. Dense technical metadata belongs in compact mono labels and secondary inspection panels. Hover effects should gently lift glass surfaces, illuminate the relevant border edge, or reveal a control’s physical affordance; click responses should translate the analyst toward a deeper inspection state rather than open unrelated decorative layers.

## Motion and Accessibility Rules

Motion is organized as **orbital context**, **acquisition**, **computation**, **evidence reveal**, and **artifact resolution**. It uses opacity, transform, and modest background-position changes only, with strong pauses between spectacular moments. The interface retains a reduced-motion mode, avoids continuously glowing every surface, and exposes status through textual labels and contrast rather than color alone. This preserves usability for technical evaluation while providing an unmistakable Earth-intelligence visual language.
